### 8. The six arcs

| Arc | Books | Central question | Major answer |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Das erste Signal | 1–5 | Where do the messages come from? | A concealed system called ECHO is still active. |
| Die verschwundenen Spuren | 6–10 | What happened to Jonas? | He is alive, investigating a cover-up, and wrongly suspects Mira. |
| Mögliche Zukünfte | 11–15 | Are these messages really the future? | They are conditional forecasts with hidden alternatives. |
| Die gelenkte Stadt | 16–20 | Why do certain predictions always come true? | Some events are being deliberately arranged. |
| Der Preis der Wahrheit | 21–25 | Can the team prove what happened? | The evidence exposes both the original cover-up and present manipulation. |
| Ein offenes Morgen | 26–30 | What should replace the secret system? | Transparent warnings and shared responsibility replace concealed control. |

## 9. The 30-book roadmap

### Arc I — Das erste Signal

#### 1. Die Nachricht um 03:17

**Case and resolution:** During a storm, Mira receives a warning about a cooling failure eleven minutes before the alarm. Her team prevents equipment damage and discovers the supposedly disconnected maintenance path.

**Character and series:** Mira initially investigates alone. Ruth’s physical inspection succeeds where the diagrams fail. An unfamiliar identifier appears in the message: ECHO.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** The operations room, light and darkness, doors, clocks, warning signs.

#### 2. Elf Minuten zu früh

**Case and resolution:** A message predicts a station announcement word for word. Suspecting clock errors, the team compares independent records. The phrase comes from a prepared emergency announcement, while the warning itself precedes the fault.

**Character and series:** Deniz must abandon his first confident explanation. The messages contain future event times, not simply incorrect timestamps.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** The station, waiting, arrival, departure, signs, directions.

#### 3. Der Schlüssel im Regen

**Case and resolution:** A key left among Jonas’s belongings opens a storage compartment in an old exchange. Someone has searched it, but Ruth finds a maintenance map hidden behind a replacement panel.

**Character and series:** Mira confronts how little she knew about her mentor’s last weeks. The map identifies a forgotten emergency route.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Keys, locks, wet clothing, hiding places, maps, searching.

#### 4. Das Haus ohne Verbindung

**Case and resolution:** A vacant municipal building appears in network records despite having no active service contract. The team discovers a maintained backup installation operating through the emergency route.

**Character and series:** Ruth recognizes work she once reported as incomplete. Her ignored report becomes valuable evidence.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Buildings, stairs, walls, basements, cables, electricity.

#### 5. Eine Stimme im Archiv

**Case and resolution:** An archived briefing contains Jonas’s voice, but its stated date conflicts with the building’s closure. The team identifies an altered catalog entry and reconstructs the recording’s original context.

**Character and series:** Leila joins the investigation and names ECHO. The local archive mystery is solved; the larger question becomes who kept the project alive.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Voices, recordings, folders, shelves, dates, memories.

### Arc II — Die verschwundenen Spuren

#### 6. Der leere Platz

**Case and resolution:** A booking places Jonas on a night train, but no reliable witness saw him aboard. Mira discovers that the ticket was bought by an automated travel process after his departure from the company.

**Character and series:** Mira begins separating evidence from assumptions. Jonas’s apparent travel history cannot be trusted.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Seats, tickets, luggage, platforms, journeys, absence.

#### 7. Das Paket aus dem Norden

**Case and resolution:** A damaged package contains an obsolete storage device and a photograph. The team identifies its sender through the pictured repair workshop and safely recovers part of a project record.

**Character and series:** Deniz slows down rather than risk destroying the only copy. The record points to an employee missing from official project lists.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Packages, wrapping, damage, photographs, tools, delivery.

#### 8. Die Frau auf der Fähre

**Case and resolution:** Mira and Ruth travel to meet former project coordinator **Anja Voss**. A mistaken meeting point makes them suspect a trap; a mundane timetable change explains the confusion.

**Character and series:** Anja confirms that Jonas is alive but fears Mira because of an approval document. Ruth persuades her to share a copy.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** The river, boats, wind, meetings, distance, trust.

#### 9. Miras Unterschrift

**Case and resolution:** Mira appears to have approved the continued operation of ECHO. Document history proves that her genuine network handover signature was attached to a different decision.

**Character and series:** Mira accepts help instead of trying to clear her name alone. The team sends verifiable evidence to Jonas through Anja.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Signatures, copies, documents, stamps, mistakes, proof.

#### 10. Die Tür am Ende des Flurs

**Case and resolution:** An invitation leads Mira to a regional research facility. Jonas is there voluntarily. Together they verify his evidence and identify why ordinary contact had broken down.

**Character and series:** Jonas returns, but Mira refuses to treat his secrecy as harmless. He reveals that an old evacuation report is central to the cover-up.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Corridors, doors, arrival, conversation, apology, return.

### Arc III — Mögliche Zukünfte

#### 11. Drei verschiedene Morgen

**Case and resolution:** Three messages describe incompatible outcomes for the same junction. Leila reconstructs their missing headers: they are separate scenarios built on different assumptions.

**Character and series:** The team gains its first firm evidence that the messages are forecasts. Mira finds uncertainty harder to accept than a single frightening answer.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Choices, paths, crossings, differences, possibilities, decisions.

#### 12. Der Fehler im Schnee

**Case and resolution:** A warning predicts a service failure during a cold spell that never reaches the city. An outdated weather input explains the failed forecast.

**Character and series:** Deniz publicly corrects an earlier conclusion. ECHO’s impressive accuracy has encouraged people to overlook ordinary data problems.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Snow, cold, warmth, clothing, shelter, changes in weather.

#### 13. Ein Fenster bleibt dunkel

**Case and resolution:** A supposedly empty building creates a contradiction in the model. Mira discovers that an informal community workshop uses it at times absent from official records.

**Character and series:** Ruth argues that people are not errors simply because a database does not describe them. The model’s blind spots acquire a human face.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Windows, rooms, neighbors, workbenches, evenings, belonging.

#### 14. Der Tag, der anders kam

**Case and resolution:** The team receives permission for a controlled test using a noncritical maintenance exercise. Changing one planned action produces a different outcome from the forecast.

**Character and series:** Mira experiences a warning as an opportunity rather than a sentence. Human decisions genuinely change what happens.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Plans, preparation, sequence, changes, cooperation, results.

#### 15. Hinter der nächsten Minute

**Case and resolution:** A partial export obscures how ECHO works. Leila and Deniz reconstruct one complete forecast, including assumptions, confidence, and alternative branches.

**Character and series:** The team conclusively rules out messages from the future. The remaining mystery is why uncertainty was deliberately removed from selected reports.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** The near future, limits, branches, details, comparison, understanding.

### Arc IV — Die gelenkte Stadt

#### 16. Der bestellte Stillstand

**Case and resolution:** A predicted service interruption matches a work order issued after the prediction. Investigation shows that a manager scheduled the disruption to produce a convincing demonstration.

**Character and series:** Mira discovers the first deliberately fulfilled forecast. The manipulation is limited but provable.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Orders, appointments, barriers, interruptions, permission, responsibility.

#### 17. Die falsche Umleitung

**Case and resolution:** A diversion repeatedly pushes traffic toward the congestion ECHO predicts. Ruth finds that the field signs follow an unauthorized revision.

**Character and series:** Ruth challenges a supervisor who dismisses her concerns. The altered route links physical instructions to the forecast team.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Roads, arrows, detours, bridges, movement, obstacles.

#### 18. Was die Stadt nicht sah

**Case and resolution:** Public dashboards show normal service while several neighborhoods report failures. Deniz traces the mismatch to filtering rules that exclude inconvenient incidents.

**Character and series:** He recognizes similarities to shortcuts he once considered harmless. Manipulation includes hiding failures, not just manufacturing success.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Neighborhoods, screens, gaps, complaints, visibility, fairness.

#### 19. Ein sicherer Fehler

**Case and resolution:** A proposed demonstration is described as harmless. Leila identifies an overlooked dependency that could affect a care facility, and the team stops the test through the responsible operators.

**Character and series:** Leila challenges Eva directly. Eva’s defense reveals her belief that public confidence justifies controlling what people see.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Safety, care, help, dependence, caution, consequences.

#### 20. Die Stadt als Versuch

**Case and resolution:** Separate incidents initially look unrelated. Combining approved schedules, field records, and forecast exports reveals a coordinated program of staged demonstrations.

**Character and series:** The team obtains evidence connecting Eva’s office to the program. They also distinguish manipulated incidents from genuine predictions and unrelated faults.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Connections, patterns, meetings, instructions, evidence, boundaries.

### Arc V — Der Preis der Wahrheit

#### 21. Das verschwundene Protokoll

**Case and resolution:** The key record from the original evacuation is missing from the central archive. Ruth locates an independently retained maintenance copy that establishes when an alternative warning was received.

**Character and series:** Her habit of preserving practical records becomes essential. The old incident can finally be reconstructed.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Records, cupboards, missing objects, searching, discovery, preservation.

#### 22. Zwei Kopien der Wahrheit

**Case and resolution:** Two apparently authentic reports describe different advice. Document histories show that both are real versions, but the later one silently removes uncertainty and an important warning.

**Character and series:** Jonas admits he once accepted the shortened report. Responsibility extends beyond a single villain.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Versions, pages, differences, omissions, comparison, admission.

#### 23. Der Zeuge am Fluss

**Case and resolution:** A retired dispatcher remembers a decision that contradicts the official account. Mira helps him compare his recollection with duty notes, separating reliable detail from uncertainty.

**Character and series:** Mira learns not to pressure a witness into the answer she wants. His evidence explains how the incomplete forecast delayed the evacuation.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Remembering, listening, benches, notebooks, doubt, courage.

#### 24. Die Nacht der offenen Fragen

**Case and resolution:** Before a formal review, the team discovers a gap in its evidence chain. An independent auditor verifies the disputed records and identifies which claims remain unproven.

**Character and series:** Mira agrees to present a narrower but stronger account. The team chooses accuracy over a dramatic accusation.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Preparation, questions, explanations, support, uncertainty, honesty.

#### 25. Was wir verschwiegen haben

**Case and resolution:** A public review reconstructs the evacuation failure, concealed project continuation, and staged incidents. Independent findings support the central allegations, and Eva loses operational authority.

**Character and series:** Leila and Jonas acknowledge their own omissions. The truth becomes public, but the city still depends on services entangled with ECHO.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Speaking, listening, audiences, consequences, accountability, relief.

### Arc VI — Ein offenes Morgen

#### 26. Am Morgen danach

**Case and resolution:** A new outage is blamed on the exposed project. The team discovers an ordinary equipment failure and restores service, showing how suspicion can distort an investigation.

**Character and series:** Mira must earn trust through clear updates rather than promises. Public disclosure has not made daily work simple.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Repair, routine, patience, tools, explanations, rebuilding.

#### 27. Die letzte Vorhersage

**Case and resolution:** A queued forecast predicts a widespread outage during ECHO’s planned retirement. Leila verifies that it is an authentic scenario, but its assumptions describe an outdated shutdown plan.

**Character and series:** The team neither obeys nor dismisses the warning. They revise the transition around the real dependency it exposes.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Endings, warnings, assumptions, preparation, transitions, care.

#### 28. Ohne geheime Wege

**Case and resolution:** A remaining undocumented connection prevents the transition from being verified. Ruth and Deniz map it to a legitimate but forgotten continuity service and arrange a documented replacement.

**Character and series:** Jonas shares everything he knows instead of managing the discovery himself. The original secret message route is retired.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Routes, labels, maps, replacement, openness, completion.

#### 29. Eine Entscheidung für alle

**Case and resolution:** During the migration rehearsal, two departments issue incompatible instructions. Mira identifies the missing decision authority and coordinates a shared plan before live services are affected.

**Character and series:** The decisive advance is cooperation, not a clever prediction. The city approves a limited forecasting service with visible assumptions and human oversight.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Agreements, roles, discussion, cooperation, choices, commitments.

#### 30. Morgen gehört niemandem

**Case and resolution:** A storm tests the new arrangements during the final transition. The team handles several plausible outcomes, restores a damaged connection, and completes the retirement without treating any forecast as destiny.

**Character and series:** Mira delegates a critical task and trusts her colleagues. The final scene returns to the Nachtwache: a new warning arrives, clearly marked as a possibility, with its assumptions visible.

**Vocabulary and visual themes:** Weather, trust, shared work, beginnings, hope, tomorrow.

The central mystery is fully resolved. The characters can have further adventures, but this series does not end with an unexplained message or a new hidden mastermind.
