# Book 01 — Die Nachricht um 03:17

Private production material; not part of the public reader.

- Series/arc: Die Nachrichten von morgen / I — Das erste Signal.
- Time: an autumn storm night in 2045; first encounter with the messages.
- Local question: is the unsolicited cooling warning real, and how did it reach a queue through a route marked disconnected?
- Resolution: a loose maintenance cover blocks a shared cooling inlet. Load reduction buys time, Ruth clears the physical obstruction, and the team prevents equipment damage. Restoration power has restarted an old gateway whose emergency maintenance circuit was never physically disconnected. The team documents the live local path, without tracing its remote origin or discovering how the advance warning was made.
- Fair clues: both cooling units share an inlet although the overview shows two units; an old amber gateway light is visible after power restoration; the incoming route carries a legacy cabinet label matching a physical cable. The retained receive log and Ruth's field observations support the resolution.
- Misleading interpretation: an old queued fault notice or a prank. The message refers to a later alarm and the actual fault is found; its advance knowledge remains a series question. Book 02 retains the independent-clock investigation.
- Emotional stake: Mira fears making a baseless report, recalling Jonas's missing support. She first investigates alone and loses useful minutes. She admits uncertainty and gives Ruth authority in the field. Their small breakfast agreement pays off the change.
- Five chapters: (1) warning at 03:17 and private checks; (2) shared intake clue, team called, alarm at 03:28; (3) physical obstruction, load reduction and teamwork; (4) safe restoration and old route traced locally; (5) morning verification, honest incident record, ECHO identifier left unexplained.
- Knowledge afterward: Mira/Deniz/Ruth know the actual fault and live local route. They have a warning received before the alarm, not proof of time travel or a forecasting system. Jonas is absent; no contact or new evidence about his whereabouts. ECHO is only an unfamiliar message identifier. Leila and Eva do not appear.
- Visual continuity: Mira short dark curls, olive jacket, silver ear stud, red notebook; Deniz black curls, round glasses, mustard sweatshirt; Ruth silver braid, orange work jacket, blue tool bag and scratched torch.
